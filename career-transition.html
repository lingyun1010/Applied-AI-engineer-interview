Background · relocation · career transition

A deliberate transition into applied AI

A factual account of the period after EMBL-EBI: first a major international relocation and family transition, then a gradual return to project work and a more intentional move towards applied AI and AI enablement.

Timeline

October–December 2025
Personal transition

Left the UK in October and spent time in China with family through December. This was a deliberate period for family and personal commitments; no professional work during this period.

Late December 2025–January 2026
Relocation and settling in

Moved to Australia at the end of December. The following weeks were occupied by settling in, meeting my partner’s family, buying a property and moving into our home. I was not working during this period.

February–June 2026
Exploration and practical projects

Started exploring AI tools and automation platforms such as Make. Alongside this, built an e-commerce website and helped local businesses improve their web pages. Also developed smaller demos and prototypes to explore practical applications of AI.

From July 2026
Focused applied-AI development

Started building a personal portfolio, which gradually evolved into EvidMatch. This marked a more deliberate focus on applied AI, including LLM-based workflows, RAG, vector search and AI application architecture. Began incorporating AI coding tools into the development workflow.

From September 2026
Local business engagement

Sharing project work and attending in-person events led to conversations with local businesses. This developed into helping businesses improve internal workflows and explore practical technology opportunities. Also began advising people from non-technical backgrounds on more structured web-project development, including architecture, security, development practices and operations.

Primary interview answer · approximately 90–120 seconds

“After leaving EMBL-EBI in October 2025, I spent the next couple of months in China with my family before relocating to Australia at the end of December.

The first few weeks in Australia were focused on settling in and personal commitments. I met my partner’s family, and we went through the process of buying a property and moving into our home. I wasn’t working during that relocation period.

From February 2026, I gradually returned to project work and started exploring AI tools and automation platforms such as Make. I also built an e-commerce website, helped local businesses improve their web pages, and experimented with smaller demos and prototypes to understand where AI could be useful in practical workflows.

In July, I became more intentional about moving towards applied AI. I started building my own portfolio, which gradually developed into EvidMatch. That gave me a practical way to deepen my understanding of LLMs, RAG, vector search and AI application architecture while building an end-to-end product.

From September onwards, sharing my work and attending local events helped me connect with local businesses. I began helping them identify opportunities to improve internal workflows, and I also started advising people from non-technical backgrounds on how to approach web projects in a more structured way—from architecture and security through to development practices and operations.

So, it has been a gradual transition: from relocation, to independent software projects, to a more focused move into applied AI and helping others adopt technology in a practical, engineering-led way.”

Short version · approximately 30 seconds

“After leaving EMBL-EBI, I took a few months to relocate from the UK to Australia and spend time with family. From February 2026, I gradually returned to project work, exploring AI automation while building websites and smaller prototypes. In July, I became more focused on applied AI and started developing my portfolio, which evolved into EvidMatch. Since September, I’ve also been working with local businesses and helping non-technical people approach digital projects in a more structured and secure way.”

中文提示：如何解释这段经历
  • 不要把2025年10月至2026年1月包装成工作或学习期。直接说明这是陪伴家人、国际搬迁、买房和安顿生活的阶段。
  • 不要说自己从2026年2月起就一直在做 AI engineering。前期是探索 AI tools、Make automation、网站项目和小型 demos/prototypes。
  • 2026年7月是更有意识地转向 Applied AI 的节点；EvidMatch 是从 portfolio 逐渐发展出来的产品。
  • 2026年9月以后增加了本地企业接触和技术指导，重点是 workflow improvement、工程化开发、architecture、security 和 operations。
  • 工具名称使用 “AI coding tools” 或 “agentic coding tools” 即可。不必主动列出某个工具的具体使用时长，也不要声称自己是某个工具的长期专家。

Likely follow-up questions

Why did you take time off after leaving EMBL-EBI?

“It was a planned personal transition. I had been working in the UK for several years, and relocating to Australia was a significant move. I wanted to spend time with family in China first, then focus on settling in Australia and establishing our home. Once that was in place, I returned to project work and began exploring the direction I wanted to take next.”

When did you decide to move into applied AI?

“I started exploring AI tools and automation in February, but the transition became much more deliberate in July. That was when I began building a portfolio around applied AI and developing the idea that became EvidMatch.”

What have you done with local businesses?

“I’ve helped businesses look at practical workflow improvements and digital processes. I’ve also supported people without a software engineering background to approach web projects more systematically—thinking about architecture, security, development workflow and how the solution will be operated, rather than just generating code.”

Accuracy boundary

This narrative deliberately does not claim continuous employment during the relocation period, years of professional AI engineering experience, or long-term expertise in any particular AI coding assistant. Add specific project names, tools or measurable outcomes only where you can explain your own contribution and defend the details under follow-up questions.
